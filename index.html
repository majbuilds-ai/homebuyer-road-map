Your homebuying road map

You do not need every answer today. Choose a stage to understand the conversation to have next.

Stage 1

Start with your goals

Think about why you want to move, when you hope to move and what matters in your next home. Separate your needs from the things that would simply be nice to have.

A useful next step

Write down your must-haves, timing and the monthly housing cost you would feel comfortable discussing with a lender.

A question to bring

What should I understand about my budget before I start looking at homes?

This is a general overview, not a fixed sequence or financing decision. Steps can overlap and vary by transaction and location. Your agent and lender can explain the details that apply to you.
